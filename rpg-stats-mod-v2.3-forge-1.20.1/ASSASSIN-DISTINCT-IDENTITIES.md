# Assassin — five flagship specialization identities

This pass sharpens five Assassin specializations that represent the clearest gameplay directions of the class. The goal is not to give each one more permanent damage; the goal is to make the button sequence and payoff feel different.

## Nightblade — ambush / vanish

Nightblade is the pure stealth assassin. A real Opening is consumed by the first melee hit. With Engine (30), that consumed Opening immediately creates a short escape window: Speed + brief Invisibility. Conversion (40) refunds a small amount of Energy. It does not receive a free second strike.

Loop: prepare Opening -> commit one strike -> disappear/reposition -> rebuild the next Opening.

## Counterblade — parry / punish

Counterblade does not chase permanent DPS. Its identity begins when a real Riposte exists. Engine (30) converts that Riposte into a brief Weakness window on the target and short Resistance on the Assassin. Conversion (40) refunds a small amount of Energy.

Loop: invite attack -> parry -> Riposte -> gain defensive tempo -> reset.

## Demolitionist — store / detonate

Demolitionist is the explosive specialist. During the demolition window it stores pressure on one target, then releases that stored amount with a hard cap. Conversion (40) adds reduced splash to at most three secondary targets and a short concussion Slow.

Loop: arm demolition -> build stored pressure -> detonate -> reposition while the internal cooldown resets.

## Soulknife — Echo / soul cut

Soulknife is the mystical-damage Assassin. Its Soul Strike consumes real Echo resource and produces a small separate magic cut with an internal cooldown. Conversion (40) turns part of the Echo cycle into Energy sustain.

Loop: build Echo -> open Soul Strike window -> spend Echo on mystical cuts -> rebuild Echo.

## Voidwalker — blink / debt / breach

Voidwalker is the spatial skirmisher. Blink creates Void Debt, which is intentionally risky because the existing late Risk layer can make the user more vulnerable while the debt is active. Connecting the follow-up hit converts the debt into movement/control: the target is slowed and the Assassin gets a short Speed window. Conversion (40) adds brief Weakness to the target and minimal Resistance to the user.

Loop: blink -> accept Void Debt -> connect the follow-up -> create space/control -> reset.

## Separation rule

- Nightblade wins by **ambush timing**.
- Counterblade wins by **defensive timing**.
- Demolitionist wins by **stored setup and area release**.
- Soulknife wins by **resource conversion into mystical secondary damage**.
- Voidwalker wins by **mobility, risk and spatial control**.

None of these five receives a new always-on raw damage multiplier. The Warrior remains the physical single-hit reference, while the Assassin reaches its best pressure through prepared windows and specialization-specific execution.
