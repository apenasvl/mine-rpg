# CHANGELOG v1.5.1 — HUD Tweak

## UI/HUD
- Reworked the combat HUD to stop occupying the middle of the screen.
- Primary resource bar (Mana / Energy / Fury etc.) moved to the lower-left corner.
- Mage tactical info (Focus / Corruption / Temporal Fragments) condensed into a compact secondary line.
- Secondary-class resource bar also uses the compact lower-left stack.
- Active skill display changed from a wide center panel to four compact slots above the hotbar.
- Skill slots now show only the bind key plus a short ability abbreviation, keeping the gameplay HUD cleaner.

## Goal
Keep the detailed RPG information in the K screen and leave the in-combat HUD minimal, readable and non-intrusive.
