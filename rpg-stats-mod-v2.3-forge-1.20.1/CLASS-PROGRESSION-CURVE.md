# RPG progression curve — difficulty contract

This project is a progression RPG. A level-1 character is intentionally incomplete and the early game is supposed to be difficult. Power should come from learning the class, unlocking its core, choosing a House and later committing to a specialization — not from receiving the endgame kit at character creation.

## Power bands

- **Levels 1–9 — survival / apprenticeship:** only the class core is being assembled. Resources are limited, mistakes matter and there is no House payoff yet.
- **Levels 10–22 — House construction:** the player chooses a House at 10 and gradually unlocks Foundation, Setup, Technique, Synergy, Signature and Mastery. The build gains reliability, but is not an endgame build.
- **Levels 23–24 — consolidation:** short bridge before specialization. House identity should already work, but the largest specialist payoffs are still unavailable.
- **Levels 25–39 — specialization comes online:** Initiation (25), Engine (30) and Technique (35) turn a general House into a real build.
- **Levels 40–49 — endgame construction:** Conversion (40), Risk (45) and Signature (48) are allowed to create stronger burst/control/sustain because the player has invested deeply.
- **Level 50 — Ascension:** the build reaches its complete form. Ascension is a short high-value window, not a permanent god mode or instant-kill button.

## Balance rules

1. Do not balance level 1 around level-50 damage. Early characters should feel limited.
2. Core nodes should mostly improve consistency/resource flow; they must not reproduce full House/spec mechanics early.
3. House mechanics may begin at level 10, but their stronger setup/signature loops are intentionally delayed.
4. Specialization-specific procs that substantially add damage/AoE should require at least Engine (30); stronger conversions should require Conversion (40).
5. Risk nodes must be conditional trade-offs, not permanent free damage.
6. Level 50 should feel clearly stronger than vanilla/netherite progression, while endgame bosses remain threatening.
7. Normal mobs are not inflated with RPG-level scaling just to fake difficulty. Early difficulty comes primarily from the player not having a completed build yet.

## Class order for the current balance pass

Mage/Iron's and Archer already have their main passes. Continue with Assassin, then Warrior, then a cross-class progression audit. Paladin is intentionally last because its damage budget must be judged after tanking, healing and support budgets are known.
