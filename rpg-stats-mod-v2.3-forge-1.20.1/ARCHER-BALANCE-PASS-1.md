# Archer balance pass 1 — persistent progression

## Goal

Make Archer progression from level 1 to 50 perceptible without overtaking Warrior as the physical single-hit benchmark. Warrior remains the reference for the hardest committed physical hit; Archer pays less commitment because it fights at range and therefore receives a smaller persistent raw-power budget.

## Persistent ranged progression

Only the Archer gets this class-specific ranged curve:

- DEX contribution: up to **+16%** at 50 Dexterity;
- RPG level contribution: up to **+7%** at level 50;
- completed core (`arc_core_mastery`): **+4%**;
- persistent ranged cap: **+27%**.

This is deliberately below the Mage's larger persistent spell progression. Archer Houses and specializations still provide their damage through conditions such as Aim, positioning, Momentum, prey marks, elemental marks and device windows.

## Why this is not a flat +27% at level 1

The three pieces grow independently. A new Archer with no Dexterity does not receive free damage. A developed Archer using the same bow sees real growth from level and Dexterity, while the final burst still requires the correct House/specialization loop.

## Physical hierarchy target

For equivalent progression/equipment quality:

1. **Warrior** — highest committed physical single hit, especially with a damage ability/window;
2. **Assassin** — can approach Warrior during a correctly prepared burst/opening, but should not sustain that output;
3. **Archer** — lower single-hit ceiling than Warrior in exchange for range, positioning and safer uptime;
4. **Paladin** — lower raw damage budget because protection/healing/support are part of its power budget.

This is a design target, not a hardcoded fixed damage number. Weapon base damage, enchantments and external mods can change displayed values.

## Existing Archer loops preserved

- Marksman: Aim and prepared shot;
- Wild Warden: prey/Instinct;
- Skirmisher: Momentum and movement;
- Arcane Archer: elemental marks/reactions;
- Artificer: device charges and prepared devices.

## Debt identified for pass 2

The audit found three mechanic issues that should be fixed separately instead of hiding them inside this numeric pass:

1. `arc_focus` is currently generated by kills/core utility but is never consumed;
2. Warden `arc_instinct` is generated and displayed by actions but has too little direct payoff;
3. Marksman Aim does not naturally decay from movement/time, so max Aim can be retained too easily.

Pass 2 should remove/repurpose Focus, make Instinct meaningful, make Aim positional rather than permanent, and then tune the five Archer Houses around the Warrior benchmark.

## Testing policy

This pass has static/CI balance contracts and a Forge build. It is not a Minecraft runtime test.
